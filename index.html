Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Photos you upload: the image only (location and camera data removed). No name, email or account.
  • Support tickets: what you type in the form, plus an optional name, email and screenshot. You get a tracking code; only a hash of it is stored.
  • Anti-abuse: a salted hash of your IP address, used for rate limits and kept for about a day. The raw IP is not stored.
  • Usage totals: five anonymous counters (visits, greeting cards created, shares, photo submissions, ticket submissions). Each event adds 1. No identifier, cookie or profile is attached. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted.

What we never collect

  • Greeting-card names and messages. Cards are made in your browser and never sent to us.
  • Advertising or cross-site tracking, or any analytics service.

Your photos

Photos are reviewed before they appear. You can report any photo, and we remove photos that break the rules. Because uploads are anonymous we cannot tell which photo is yours; to ask for removal, open a support ticket and describe the photo.
